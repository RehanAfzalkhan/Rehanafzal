Current research

Computer vision · ICMLA 2026

Fingerphoto Deblurring Using Wavelet-Guided TransformerNet

Restoring blurred contactless fingerprints while preserving the ridge structure needed for biometric recognition.

Our framework combines a Stationary Wavelet Packet Transform (SWPT), Whitening and Coloring Transform (WCT) guidance, and a Restormer-style encoder–decoder. Identity-aware supervision complements image reconstruction.

Generative AI · Ongoing research

Identity-preserving controllable face generation

Investigating how diffusion models can preserve a person’s identity while following edits to pose, expression, age, and context. The research explores instruction-aware identity guidance and evaluation that distinguishes identity preservation from copying the reference image.

Research tools · Image processing

Wavelet analysis for image restoration

Developed notebooks for exploring multi-level Haar wavelets, including level-three wavelet packet decomposition into 64 subbands. Visualizations, coefficient statistics, covariance analysis, and CSV exports support the study of image structure for fingerprint restoration.

Tools: PyWavelets, OpenCV, NumPy, pandas, Matplotlib

Machine learning · Coursework

Machine learning from scratch

Implemented logistic regression and decision trees in NumPy and compared them with scikit-learn on the Wisconsin Diagnostic Breast Cancer dataset. The workflow includes EDA, stratified train/validation/test splits, training-only scaling, and validation-based tuning. The saved notebook reports 97.67% logistic-regression accuracy on 86 held-out test samples.

Tools: NumPy, pandas, scikit-learn, Matplotlib
